One status update, at most 120 characters, in the voice of a lost sock —
for the [Funniest Lost Sock Status Update](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-lost-sock-status-update-4b1fa3ce)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The line

> Status update: the dog has me in his mouth. Again. He calls it love. I call it a hostage situation with drool.

110 characters. The sock is not behind the dryer and has not lost its
partner — it has been adopted, against its will, as a chew toy, and it
has filed its complaint as a status update.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`)
with no trailing newline, and prints it.
